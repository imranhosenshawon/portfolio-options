Research Output

📄 Published Article · 2025
Effect of Temperature on Drying Kinetics, Physico-Chemical Properties and Antioxidant Activity of Cabinet Dried Whole Jujubes

Empirical investigation of how drying temperature affects the quality parameters and antioxidant retention of whole jujubes during cabinet drying — with implications for food preservation optimisation.

DOI: 10.53430/ijmru.2025.9.2.0021 →
🔄 Under Review
Effect of Microwave Heating on Quality Attributes of Sugarcane Juice During Refrigerated Storage

Submitted to the Journal of Food and Nutrition Research. Evaluated how microwave heating affects selected quality attributes of sugarcane juice across refrigerated storage periods — an applied study on thermal processing and quality retention.

🎓 MSc Dissertation · 2025–2026
Consumer Perceptions towards Smart Packaging in Processed Food Items in Bangladesh

Quantitative study investigating consumer awareness, attitudes and behavioural intentions towards smart packaging technologies in Bangladesh. Designed structured survey via Qualtrics; analysis conducted with IBM SPSS.

Gelatin-CMC Based Biodegradable Packaging Films for Baked Products

University Grants Commission-funded project at HSTU. Developed and laboratory-evaluated synthetic and extracted gelatin-CMC packaging films, investigating their physicochemical properties and suitability for baked food packaging.
